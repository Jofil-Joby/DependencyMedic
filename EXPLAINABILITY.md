## Decision and Reasoning

DependencyMedic makes an assessment by analyzing evidence related to project dependencies. It connects detected problems to supporting evidence and practical actions.

## Inputs and Data Sources

DependencyMedic uses source files, configuration, project structure, and relevant project dependencies data from the inspected project.

## Limits and Constraints

DependencyMedic is limited when required information is missing, inaccessible, generated dynamically, or incomplete.
